Double bass samples: **Meatbass** by Karoryfer Samples (a 1958 Otto Rubner double bass played and mapped by Drogomir Smolken, recorded by Ludwik Zamenhof), CC0 1.0.
Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.meatbass (pizzicato, velocity layer 3, round robin 1).
Changes: cut to 2.6 s, converted to mono MP3. Sampled every minor third; Jazzlab pitch-shifts to the notes in between.
`map.json` holds each sample's tuning (cents, measured) and level (dB).
